## About Me

I am Bolun Li (李博伦), currently a Ph.D. student at the **School of Computer Science, Sun Yat-sen University**, since September 2026.

Prior to that, I received my M.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology from **Fuzhou University** in June 2026 and my B.Eng. degree in Software Engineering from the **University of South China** in June 2023.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **hardware security, reinforcement learning, and deep learning**. My previous research primarily focused on hardware Trojan detection, particularly identifying malicious circuitry in incomplete or partially reconstructed netlists.

## Research Interests

- Hardware Security
- Hardware Trojan Detection
- Deep Learning
